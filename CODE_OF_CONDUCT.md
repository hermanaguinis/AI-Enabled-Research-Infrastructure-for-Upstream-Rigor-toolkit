# Code of Conduct

This project exists to operationalize methodological best practice, which means contributors will sometimes disagree about what "best practice" is. Disagreement is expected and welcome; it must be conducted respectfully and grounded in evidence.

## Expected behavior

- Ground methodological disagreement in cited, peer-reviewed evidence rather than personal opinion or seniority.
- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are about the state of the methodological literature, not about people.
- Be direct but respectful in issues, pull requests, and reviews.
- Give newcomers -- including doctoral students and researchers outside major research institutions -- the same consideration given to established scholars.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Personal attacks, harassment, or dismissiveness toward contributors, including on the basis of career stage, institution, or nationality.
- Asserting a methodological correction without citing supporting evidence.
- Attempting to introduce server-side data collection, tracking, or any change that would compromise a tool's auditability or privacy-by-design model.

## Enforcement

Reports of unacceptable behavior can be raised via a GitHub issue or directly to the repository maintainer. See `docs/governance.md` for how methodological and conduct disputes are resolved when they cannot be settled through the normal pull-request process.
