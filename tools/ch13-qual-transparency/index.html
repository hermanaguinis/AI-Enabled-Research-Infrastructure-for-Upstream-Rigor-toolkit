Primary source. Aguinis, H., & Solarino, A. M. 2019. Transparency and replicability in qualitative research: The case of interviews with elite informants. Strategic Management Journal, 40(8): 1291–1315.
Recommended companion. Aguinis, H. 2025. Research methodology: Best practices for rigorous, credible, and impactful research. SAGE. collegepublishing.sagepub.com/products/research-methodology-1-280088
This tool operationalizes the 12 criteria and 4-point BARS from Aguinis & Solarino (2019). Transparency is a cumulative continuum: no single criterion holds veto power over publication, and criteria that do not fit a study's design or ontology may be marked Not applicable with a justification. The criteria extend Lincoln & Guba's (1985) notion of "thick description" and apply to purely qualitative and mixed-methods research alike.
