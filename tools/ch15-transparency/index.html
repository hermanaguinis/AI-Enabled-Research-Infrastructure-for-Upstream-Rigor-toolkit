Methodological Transparency Companion

Document the choices, judgment calls, and decisions behind your study so others can reproduce your inferences. Transparency is a continuum: the more you disclose about what happens in the "research kitchen," the greater the inferential reproducibility, credibility, and replicability of your results. This is about disclosure, not judging whether a choice was right.
