Policy makers

Society

Influence on legislators and government officials who shape policy for the public good.

Core principles the toolkit is built on

  • Impact on one dimension does not imply impact on the others — each needs its own measure.
  • Be locally sensitive: institutions and individuals have different strategic priorities that shift over time.
  • The dimensions are complementary, not mutually exclusive.
  • A single action can affect multiple dimensions and stakeholders simultaneously.
  • The model is flexible, expandable, scalable, and adaptable — so you can add or reweight dimensions to fit your context.

About the Scholarly Impact Toolkit

This is a practical companion for improving the impact of research. It translates a validated, multidimensional and multistakeholder model of scholarly impact into interactive modules, trackable actions, and an exportable action plan.

Scholarly impact is defined across four dimensions — Theory & Research (other researchers), Education (students), Organizations (practitioners), and Society (policy makers). Impact on one dimension does not imply impact on the others; the dimensions are complementary, and single actions can touch several at once. The model is flexible, expandable, scalable, and adaptable to different institutions and career stages.
