Interactive methodological literature review tool

Builder, Evaluator, and Teaching Toolkit

A self-contained workspace for planning, conducting, writing, revising, reviewing, and teaching best-in-kind methodological literature reviews. It operationalizes review type choice, the four broad issues, the ten-domain checklist, transparent scoring, risk diagnosis, and report generation.

Source: Aguinis, H. 2025. Research methodology: Best practices for rigorous, credible, and impactful research. SAGE. https://collegepublishing.sagepub.com/products/research-methodology-1-280088

Four Broad-Issue Workflow

Use these sections to make judgment calls explicit before drafting, during revision, or while reviewing a published/submitted methodological literature review.

Ten-Domain Checklist and Scoring Rubric

Score every observable indicator: 0 = absent, 1 = weak, 2 = adequate, 3 = strong, or N/A. Evidence quality matters: a section heading does not earn credit if it lacks transparency, specificity, or evidentiary support.

Low scores in scope, process, or source-of-recommendations will increase trustworthiness risk because they weaken transparency and replicability.

Teaching prompts

Can students identify the review type and explain why it fits the purpose?
Can students trace each major recommendation to an evidentiary source?
Can students identify hidden judgment calls or scope limitations?
Can students convert recommendations into an applied checklist or protocol?
Can students identify possible QRPs the review helps prevent?
