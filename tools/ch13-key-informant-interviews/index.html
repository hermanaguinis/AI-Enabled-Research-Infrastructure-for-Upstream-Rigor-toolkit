Method fit diagnostic

Use interviews with key informants only when they add information that cannot be obtained adequately from less costly or less access-dependent sources.

About this tool

This tool helps researchers plan and report interview research with key informants: individuals who are key decision makers, possess extensive and exclusive information, and can influence important organizational outcomes, either alone or jointly with others.

Based on: Aguinis, H. 2025. Research methodology: Best practices for rigorous, credible, and impactful research. SAGE. https://collegepublishing.sagepub.com/products/research-methodology-1-280088

Mode comparison and recommendation

In person

Strong for rapport, nonverbal cues, and sensitive interpretation. Costly and schedule-dependent.

Video

Strong offline alternative when geography or travel blocks access. Preserves some nonverbal information.

Phone

Flexible and sometimes less status-laden, but weak on nonverbal cues and harder to manage drift.
