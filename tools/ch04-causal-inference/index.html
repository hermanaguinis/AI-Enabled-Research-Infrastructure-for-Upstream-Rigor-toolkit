Causal Inference Research Planning Tool

An interactive guide for planning rigorous, credible, relevant, and causally interpretable social and behavioral science research.

Source credit: Based on Aguinis, H. 2025. Research methodology: Best practices for rigorous, credible, and impactful research. SAGE. https://collegepublishing.sagepub.com/products/research-methodology-1-280088
Core principle: Causal inference is first a design problem. Weak research design cannot be fixed after data collection by sophisticated statistical analysis.
✓ Practical relevance: identify important research questions, beneficiaries, and actionable decision consequences.
✓ Causal design: test association, temporal precedence, and rival explanations before data collection.
✓ Design selection: compare experiments, quasi-experiments, matching, IVs, ESM, eLancing, and VR.
✓ Validity balance: diagnose internal and external validity tradeoffs.
✓ Control variables: include, exclude, or reclassify controls using theoretical and measurement logic.
✓ Manuscript readiness: generate transparent language for methods, controls, and limitations.

11. ESM, eLancing, and VR design recommender

Match the research question to a methodological tool that can help balance internal and external validity.

Experience Sampling Methodology

  • Captures experiences, attitudes, emotions, and behaviors over time.
  • Useful for temporal sequencing and within-person causal ordering.
  • Natural settings support external validity.

eLancing field experiments

  • Online labor markets support field experiments.
  • Allows random assignment in a natural work environment.
  • Useful for manipulating tasks, teams, and information availability.

Virtual reality

  • Immersive simulations for sensitive, rare, or ethically difficult topics.
  • Allows random assignment and controlled realism.
  • Useful for studying reactions in realistic but safe environments.
