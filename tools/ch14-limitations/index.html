The 8 guidelines

  • 1
    Make it a priority — report at least one limitation.
  • 2
    Use a separate, labeled section.
  • 3
    Note that reviewers/editors share responsibility.
  • 4
    Focus on weaknesses that matter; prioritize.
  • 5
    Highlight the "so what," not just the "what."
  • 6
    Describe each limitation — do not justify it away.
  • 7
    Future research: immediate & incremental.
  • 8
    Use future research to advance theory.
